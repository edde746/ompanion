ompanion 2.1.0 answers omp's form questions in the chat, opens an agent page's pictures in a zoomable image viewer, and reconnects at once when the app comes back to the front. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

## New

- Form questions: from omp 18.18.3 the `ask` tool can ask with one HTML form, and the chat shows the whole form in place: choices with Other, menus, text fields, text, lists, code, tables and pictures. Submit checks your answers by omp's rules and names the problem under each field, and the transcript lists the answers
- Single-choice and multiple-choice questions look apart: one choice is a pill in a shared track, several are separate tiles with check boxes and a count of the chosen ones, and Other is the last row with a field for your answer
- Agent pages: a click on a picture opens it in the image viewer
- The image viewer zooms: a bar with zoom out, the zoom against the image's own pixels, zoom in and fit to window; double tap for the image's pixels and back; and the `+`, `-` and `0` keys
- When the interface hits an error it cannot recover from, it says so with the last errors, Copy details and Reload, instead of a screen that no longer reacts

## Changed

- Following a running session takes far less data: a run's log arrives compressed, 1.3 MB instead of 4.8 MB on a first open of a busy session

## Fixed

- When the app comes back to the front it checks every machine's connection, so one that died in the background (another network, sockets the system reclaimed) reconnects within seconds instead of the chat showing nothing new for up to a minute
- A chat that fell behind while the app was suspended no longer stays at an old message until the app restarts
- Large sessions no longer reconnect with "channel failed" every few seconds: the Tree tab reads a slim tree (3.6 MB instead of 30 MB for one 26 MB session), and only while it shows
- A fling through a long task keeps its place, and on iOS its momentum, instead of jumping back
- Mention chips sit on the text's baseline
