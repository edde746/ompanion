ompanion 1.2.0 works with omp 18.10 and installs omp 18.10.3 on a machine that lacks it.

## New

- A Stats tab on the usage pane: every machine's omp sessions summed into totals, a contribution grid with streaks, an activity chart, a week-by-hour grid, and model, project, tool, agent and machine lists
- Prompt stats on the Stats tab: words, questions, tone, prompt lengths, the longest prompt, prompts by hour, and a word cloud of the words your prompts use most
- iPhone and iPad: attach from the photo library, and paste an image copied in another app into the composer
- The agent hub lists active and inactive agents apart, newest first

## Changed

- Chat images load once a scroll settles on them, so a fling no longer loads every image it passes, and thumbnails decode at the size they show
- An account's policy line on the usage pane says when its quota is exhausted, as omp 18.10 does

## Fixed

- With omp 18.8.7 and later, sessions started from a device get a title again
- A provider you sign in to, or whose key you store, while a session runs can be used in that session; before, picking its model failed with "Model not found"
- A folded turn keeps every reply the model finished with, so an answer stays when a background job's result starts another run
- Windows: after switching back to the window, the next key no longer replaces a field's whole text
