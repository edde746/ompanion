ompanion 2.0.1 polishes the 2.0 app after a review of every screen, and fixes races in sessions, goals and connections. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

## Changed

- Animations play faster, and menus fade in instead of unrolling row by row
- A run waiting for your answer says so: `Needs your input` in the chat header, and a mark on the tool call waiting for approval
- Approvals: approve once is the one filled button; buttons that do not fit in one row stack into a column, and a long request scrolls above them with a fade at its edge
- The usage page is tidier: one toolbar line, a row only for the limits an account reports, and machines listed only when they need attention
- Empty places say what to do next: a machine without sessions offers a new one, the home page offers to install omp on a machine that lacks it, and the terminal tab offers a new terminal
- Files: a breadcrumb shows the current folder from `~` or `/`, wrapped lines start past their own indent, and a diff starts at its first hunk
- The session tree marks the current position and dims the branches off its path
- Sidebar: each session status has its own mark, and a session open in omp on the machine shows a terminal mark
- Phones: message actions take a line of their own instead of narrowing the message, and Settings no longer lists keyboard shortcuts
- Notices sit above the composer, wrap to at most three lines, and stay under open dialogs
- German: the strings added in 2.0.0 read consistently and address you as "du"

## Fixed

- macOS: text is drawn at its normal weight again; 2.0.0 drew every weight thinner, worst on dark themes
- macOS: text fields no longer autocorrect or underline spelling
- Scrolling up in a chat while a reply streams is no longer pulled back to the bottom, and on iOS keeps its momentum
- The context meter shows the figure omp's own footer shows
- Goals continue after a compaction instead of waiting for you, and goals, loops, titles and the idle exit of detached runs stay consistent when events race
- A result that arrives after you moved on (another session, a closed dialog, an edited machine) no longer lands on the wrong screen, and syntax highlighting that failed to load loads again
- An SSH key agent that never answers no longer holds a connection past its timeout
- A reply's footer and its thinking line no longer show times of zero, and an attached folder shows as a folder in its message
