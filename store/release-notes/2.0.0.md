ompanion 2.0.0 is the same app rebuilt: a web UI in each platform's own webview, on a Go core, in place of Flutter. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later, 18.16 included, and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

## Moving to 2.0.0

- Your machines, keys, trusted host keys, passwords, settings, pinned sessions and read markers come with you: on its first launch the app imports them from 1.x, and keeps the old database as `ompanion.sqlite.imported`
- iPhones and iPads need iOS 16, Macs macOS 13; on iOS 15 and macOS 12 the app stays at 1.2.1
- Smaller downloads: about 10 MB on Android instead of 35, 9 MB for the Mac instead of 28

## New

- Agent pages: the agent can show an HTML page in the chat (a chart, a table, a small tool) with the `html_render` tool. It runs sandboxed, opens full screen and saves as HTML, and its links ask before they open
- German, and a language setting
- Usage reminders: a notification when a limit resets or an account is usable again
- The model pickers list your recently used models first
- Hiding account details on the usage page covers emails and organizations with a random mosaic, for screenshots
- SSH keys moved from the sidebar into Settings
- Android 14 and later: the back gesture shows the page behind as you swipe

## Fixed

- omp 18.16: the companion loads again, so goals, loops, pause, queue edits, settings, push and the idle exit of detached runs work (#6)
- Ubuntu 26.04 machines: two devices sending to a session at once no longer both take its lock
- Stopping a command on this computer also stops what it started
- Phones: the model list stays above the composer and the keyboard, and the composer's buttons sit on the screen's insets
- The `ask` tool's questions can be selected and their links open
