ompanion 2.1.0 answers omp's form questions in the chat, opens an agent page's pictures in a zoomable image viewer, and reconnects at once when the app comes back to the front. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

New
- Form questions: from omp 18.18.3 the ask tool can ask with one form, and the chat shows the whole form in place: choices, menus, text fields, lists, tables and pictures. Submit checks your answers and names the problem under each field
- Single-choice and multiple-choice questions look apart: one choice is a pill in a shared track, several are separate tiles with check boxes and a count of the chosen ones
- Agent pages: a tap on a picture opens it in the image viewer
- The image viewer zooms: a bar with zoom out, actual size, zoom in and fit, and double tap for the image's pixels and back
- When the interface hits an error it cannot recover from, it says so with Copy details and Reload, instead of a screen that no longer reacts

Changed
- Following a running session takes far less data: a run's log arrives compressed

Fixed
- When the app comes back to the front it checks every machine's connection, so one that died in the background reconnects within seconds instead of the chat showing nothing new for up to a minute
- A chat that fell behind while the app was suspended no longer stays at an old message until the app restarts
- Large sessions no longer reconnect with "channel failed" every few seconds
- A fling through a long task keeps its place and its momentum instead of jumping back
- Mention chips sit on the text's baseline
