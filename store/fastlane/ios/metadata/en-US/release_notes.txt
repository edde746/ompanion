ompanion 2.0.1 polishes the 2.0 app after a review of every screen, and fixes races in sessions, goals and connections. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

Changed
- Animations play faster, and menus fade in instead of unrolling row by row
- A run waiting for your answer says so: "Needs your input" in the chat header, and a mark on the tool call waiting for approval
- Approvals: approve once is the one filled button; buttons that do not fit in one row stack into a column, and a long request scrolls above them
- The usage page is tidier: one toolbar line, a row only for the limits an account reports, and machines listed only when they need attention
- A machine without sessions offers a new one, the home page offers to install omp on a machine that lacks it, and the terminal tab offers a new terminal
- Files: a breadcrumb shows the current folder, wrapped lines start past their own indent, and a diff starts at its first hunk
- The session tree marks the current position and dims the branches off its path
- Message actions take a line of their own on iPhone instead of narrowing the message
- Notices sit above the composer and stay under open dialogs
- German: the strings added in 2.0.0 read consistently

Fixed
- Scrolling up in a chat while a reply streams is no longer pulled back to the bottom, and keeps its momentum
- The context meter shows the figure omp's own footer shows
- Goals continue after a compaction instead of waiting for you
- A result that arrives after you moved on (another session, a closed dialog) no longer lands on the wrong screen, and syntax highlighting that failed to load loads again
