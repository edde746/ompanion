ompanion 2.0 is the same app rebuilt on the system's own web view, a smaller download. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later, 18.16 included, and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it. It needs iOS 16.

Moving to 2.0
- Your machines, keys, trusted host keys, passwords, settings, pinned sessions and read markers come with you: the app imports them on its first launch

New
- Agent pages: the agent can show an HTML page in the chat (a chart, a table, a small tool). It runs sandboxed, opens full screen and saves as HTML, and its links ask before they open
- German, and a language setting
- Usage reminders: a notification when a limit resets or an account is usable again
- The model pickers list your recently used models first
- Hiding account details on the usage page covers emails and organizations with a random mosaic, for screenshots
- SSH keys moved into Settings

Fixed
- omp 18.16: the companion loads again, so goals, loops, pause, queue edits, settings, push and the idle exit of detached runs work
- Ubuntu 26.04 machines: two devices sending to a session at once no longer both take its lock
- The model list stays above the composer and the keyboard, and the composer's buttons sit on the screen's insets
- The ask tool's questions can be selected and their links open
