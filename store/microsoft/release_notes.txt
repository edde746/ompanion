Works with omp 18.10, and installs omp 18.10.3 on a machine that lacks it.

New: a Stats tab on the usage pane sums every machine's omp sessions into totals, a contribution grid with streaks, an activity chart, a week-by-hour grid, and model, project, tool, agent and machine lists, plus prompt stats and a word cloud of the words your prompts use most. The agent hub lists active and inactive agents apart, newest first.

Changed: chat images load once a scroll settles on them, and thumbnails decode at the size they show. An account's policy line on the usage pane says when its quota is exhausted, as omp 18.10 does.

Fixed: after switching back to the window, the next key no longer replaces a field's whole text. With omp 18.8.7 and later, sessions started from a device get a title again. A provider you sign in to while a session runs can be used in that session. A folded turn keeps every reply the model finished with.
