ompanion 2.0.1 polishes the 2.0 app after a review of every screen, and fixes races in sessions, goals and connections. It works with omp 18.3.1 and later and installs omp 18.16.0 on a machine that lacks it.

Changed: animations play faster, and menus fade in. A run waiting for your answer says "Needs your input" in the chat header and marks the tool call waiting for approval. Approve once is the one filled button, and approval buttons stack into a column when they do not fit in one row. The usage page is tidier and lists only the machines that need attention. Empty places say what to do next: a new session, installing omp, a new terminal. The file browser shows a breadcrumb, the editor indents wrapped lines past their own indent, and a diff starts at its first hunk. Notices sit above the composer and under open dialogs.

Fixed: scrolling up while a reply streams is no longer pulled back to the bottom. The context meter shows the figure omp's own footer shows. Goals continue after a compaction instead of waiting for you. A result that arrives after you moved on no longer lands on the wrong screen, and syntax highlighting that failed to load loads again. An SSH key agent that never answers no longer holds a connection past its timeout.
