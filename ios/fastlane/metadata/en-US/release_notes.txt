Works with omp 18.10, and installs omp 18.10.3 on a machine that lacks it.

New
- A Stats tab on the usage pane: every machine's omp sessions summed into totals, a contribution grid with streaks, an activity chart, a week-by-hour grid, and model, project, tool, agent and machine lists
- Prompt stats: words, questions, tone, prompt lengths, the longest prompt, prompts by hour, and a word cloud of the words your prompts use most
- Attach from the photo library: the attach button offers Photo Library and Files
- Paste an image copied in another app into the composer
- The agent hub lists active and inactive agents apart, newest first

Changed
- Chat images load once a scroll settles on them, and thumbnails decode at the size they show
- An account's policy line on the usage pane says when its quota is exhausted, as omp 18.10 does

Fixed
- With omp 18.8.7 and later, sessions started from your iPhone or iPad get a title again
- A provider you sign in to, or whose key you store, while a session runs can be used in that session
- A folded turn keeps every reply the model finished with, so an answer stays when a background job's result starts another run
